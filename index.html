Profile & Story

// the person behind the lectures

Ikraam moved from Port Elizabeth → Pretoria to "try it out" — and after lecturing his first class, it just stuck.

Now a Junior Lecturer and winner of Lecturer of the Year 2025, Ikraam believes engagement is everything. No preaching from the front. Just real discussions, practical exercises, and helping students surpass him.

His superpower? Always being willing to help.

His teaching philosophy? Keep it interactive, keep it real — and never stop learning.
